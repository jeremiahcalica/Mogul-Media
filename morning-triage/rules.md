# Standing rules from Jeremiah

Rules about which sources to trust. These are not failure modes (those are in
failure-modes.md) and not ownership (that is roster.md). Quote his words and
cite the case each rule came from.

## R-001 (2026-10-24) — Ignore Kyle's EOD topic list for due dates

Jeremiah: "Mason L and Joshua C. are due on Monday, Lior P. by Tuesday, don't
mind Kyle's EOD list".

Case: the "KYLE EOD REPORT" of 2026-10-23 in #mogul-media-strategists
(https://mogulmediaworkspace.slack.com/archives/C087TABLXU1/p1792799639567549)
listed Mason, Josh C and Lior under "TOPIC CREATION (JEREMIAH)" as "(Due
Thursday)". The real deadlines were Mon Oct 26 and Tue Oct 27, and they match
the due dates on the ClickUp "OCT WK5 | New Topics" tasks exactly.

Rule: the topic deadline is the ClickUp "New Topics" task's due date. Kyle's
EOD "TOPIC CREATION" lines are never a task trigger and never a due date.

## R-002 (2026-10-24) — Internal asks addressed to him by name are his

Asked: "Should internal asks addressed to you by name become Notion tasks?"
Jeremiah: "True".

Case: the 2026-10-24 run held back Devin's training action items, Martin's and
Claudia's DOS feedback asks, and Claudia's Monday-agenda question, because they
have no client.

Rule: an internal ask (no client) that names Jeremiah, by @-mention or in a list
of action items for him, is MINE. The Notion title is `Internal | <verb +
deliverable>`, with Role `INTERNAL`. An internal ask addressed to "team",
"@here" or nobody is still NEEDS REVIEW. Ranking is unchanged: a direct
unanswered question from Kyle, Devin or ops is P1; a designer's question or an
internal promise with a date is P2.

## R-003 (2026-10-24) — Runway alerts never create a task

Asked: "Do runway alerts on your writer clients mean anything for you to do?"
Jeremiah: "Runway? no."

Case: PMwazowski's "The client is out of content and requires an immediate
update." on Caulen F, Michael K, Nathan C, Shane H and Teddy T, and "Please
review and update the content." on Mason L, all on 2026-10-23.

Rule: content-runway alerts are never MINE and never Needs review. They are
dropped and counted in the run log. **This overrides the original brief's P0
rule** ("one of my WRITER clients whose content runway has expired or ends
today"). Runway also stops being a tie-breaker. Ties go to earliest due date,
then oldest unanswered ping.

## R-004 (2026-10-24) — New Topics: both of them tagged

Asked: "New Topics task always yours, even when only Kyle is assigned?"
Jeremiah: "both of us have to be tagged".

Rule:
- A ClickUp "New Topics" task with **both Jeremiah and Kyle assigned**, on a
  BOTH or SA-TOPICS client, is MINE. Its ClickUp due date is the deadline
  (R-001).
- With **only Kyle** assigned, it is NEEDS REVIEW, never MINE. The question
  for Jeremiah: "Kyle-only on ClickUp. Yours?"

Case and the one exception: the three OCT WK5 New Topics tasks (`12439gntem6`
Mason, `12439gntf2q` Josh, `12439gntf6q` Lior) have only Kyle assigned. They
became Notion rows because Jeremiah confirmed each by name with a due date, not
through this rule. They stay unless he says otherwise.
